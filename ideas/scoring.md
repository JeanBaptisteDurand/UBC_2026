# Scoring the ideas

First pass, our own opinion. 1 = weak, 5 = strong.

## Criteria

- **Problem**: is the pain real and can we name who has it?
- **Chain needed**: would it work just as well without a blockchain?
- **Demo**: can a judge see it work in 20 seconds?
- **Doable**: can two people ship it in 7 days?
- **Awards**: how many prizes can it stack (track + Arc + Official Winner + bounties)?

## First pass

| Idea | Problem | Chain needed | Demo | Doable | Awards |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verifiable compute marketplace (enclaves) | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| Sealed bug bounty | 4 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| Real vs displayed yield + fake token guard | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Pay-per-call APIs for agents | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Rent deposit escrow | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| Prediction market companion | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |

## Shortlist

1. Verifiable compute marketplace, with the sealed bug bounty as its first product
2. Real vs displayed yield + fake token guard
3. Prediction market companion (only if we go for the Gemini bounty)
